Core Organization

Rocketry Division

Our current flagship division is our rocketry division. This team is dedicated towards designing, testing, and launching our club rocketry projects as well as educating students in solid rocketry and supporting these rocketeers as they progress in the sport.

The Rocketry Division at UofT STAR is the only rocketry group run by students of the Faculty of Arts & Science (FAS). Our goal is to be the go-to rocketry team for anybody at the University looking to learn about or find a community for rocketeers.

AIAA

UofT STAR is pleased to announce this year that it is working with the renewed UofT AIAA Chapter as a new subdivision of the team. The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics is a North American organization dedicated towards education, outreach, and networking for various topics in Aerospace Engineering. You can check out more about our AIAA team on its own page for information, activities, and future updates. Visit the AIAA page to follow that side of the organization.
